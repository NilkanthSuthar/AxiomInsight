# Quarterly Financial Report - Q4 2024
## Axiom Company - Canadian Operations

## Executive Summary

Axiom Company's Canadian operations demonstrated strong financial performance in Q4 2024, with revenue reaching CAD $12.4M, representing a 18% year-over-year growth. Operating margins improved to 24.3%, driven by operational efficiencies and strategic cost management.
