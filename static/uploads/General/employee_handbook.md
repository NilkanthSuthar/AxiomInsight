## Employment Policies

### Equal Opportunity Employment

Axiom Company is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, or any other protected characteristic under Canadian federal and provincial laws.

We comply with:
- Canadian Human Rights Act
- Employment Equity Act
- Ontario Human Rights Code (and equivalent provincial legislation)
- Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA)

### Employment Classification

**Full-Time Employees:** 37.5 hours per week, eligible for full benefits  
**Part-Time Employees:** Less than 30 hours per week, pro-rated benefits  
**Contract Employees:** Fixed-term contracts, as specified in agreement  
**Interns/Co-op Students:** Temporary positions, typically 4-12 months

### Probationary Period

All new employees undergo a 90-day probationary period. During this time, either party may terminate employment with one week's notice. Performance reviews occur at 30, 60, and 90 days.

### Background Checks

Conditional offers of employment are subject to:
- Criminal record check (where permitted by law)
- Education and employment verification
- Professional reference checks
- Credit check (for finance positions only)

## Compensation & Benefits

### Salary Structure

**Salary Reviews:** Annual reviews in January, effective February 1  
**Performance Bonuses:** Based on individual and company performance  
**Pay Schedule:** Bi-weekly direct deposit (every second Friday)  
**Overtime:** Non-exempt employees receive 1.5x regular rate for hours over 44/week (Ontario)

### Benefits Package (Full-Time Employees)

#### Health & Wellness
- **Extended Health Care:** 100% employer-paid, family coverage available
- **Dental Care:** 80% employer-paid, orthodontics included
- **Vision Care:** $500 per person every 2 years
- **Mental Health Support:** $5,000 annual coverage for counseling/therapy
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** 24/7 confidential support

#### Insurance
- **Life Insurance:** 2x annual salary, employer-paid
- **Accidental Death & Dismemberment:** 2x annual salary
- **Short-Term Disability:** 70% of salary after 1 week, up to 26 weeks
- **Long-Term Disability:** 60% of salary after 26 weeks
- **Critical Illness Insurance:** $50,000 coverage

#### Retirement Savings
- **RRSP Matching:** Employer matches 100% up to 5% of salary
- **Vesting:** Immediate
- **Investment Options:** Self-directed through Wealthsimple

#### Additional Perks
- **Professional Development:** $3,000 annual allowance
- **Gym Membership:** $600 annual subsidy
- **Transit Pass:** 100% coverage for public transportation
- **Home Office Stipend:** $1,200 annual allowance
- **Technology Allowance:** $2,500 every 3 years for personal device
- **Phone/Internet:** $100 monthly reimbursement for remote workers

## Work Hours & Time Off

### Standard Work Hours

**Office Hours:** 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM (with 1-hour lunch break)  
**Core Hours:** 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (must be available)  
**Flexible Hours:** Allowed with manager approval

### Hybrid Work Policy

- **In-Office Days:** Minimum 2 days per week (Tuesday & Wednesday recommended)
- **Remote Work:** Up to 3 days per week
- **Fully Remote:** Available for certain roles with VP approval
- **Work from Anywhere:** Up to 4 weeks per year from anywhere in Canada

### Vacation & Paid Time Off

#### Vacation Entitlement
- **Years 1-2:** 15 days (3 weeks)
- **Years 3-5:** 20 days (4 weeks)
- **Years 6-10:** 25 days (5 weeks)
- **Years 11+:** 30 days (6 weeks)

**Vacation Year:** January 1 - December 31  
**Carryover:** Maximum 5 days to following year  
**Payout on Termination:** Unused vacation paid out

#### Statutory Holidays (2025)
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Family Day (February 17)
- Good Friday (April 18)
- Victoria Day (May 19)
- Canada Day (July 1)
- Civic Holiday (August 4)
- Labour Day (September 1)
- Thanksgiving (October 13)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- Boxing Day (December 26)

**Total:** 10 paid statutory holidays

#### Additional Paid Time Off
- **Personal Days:** 5 days per year (no questions asked)
- **Sick Days:** Unlimited (reasonable use expected)
- **Bereavement Leave:** Up to 5 days for immediate family
- **Parental Leave:** Top-up to 85% of salary for 17 weeks (primary caregiver)
- **Volunteer Days:** 2 paid days per year for charitable work

### Parental Leave

**Maternity Leave:** Up to 17 weeks, Axiom tops up EI to 85% of salary  
**Parental Leave:** Up to 35 weeks (can be shared between parents)  
**Adoption Leave:** Same as parental leave  
**Return to Work:** Guaranteed position or equivalent upon return

## Workplace Conduct

### Code of Conduct

All employees must:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Report unethical behavior through appropriate channels

### Anti-Harassment & Discrimination Policy

Axiom Company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind. This includes:
- Sexual harassment
- Racial discrimination
- Gender-based discrimination
- Age discrimination
- Disability discrimination
- Any form of workplace bullying

**Reporting:** Incidents should be reported to HR immediately. All complaints are investigated confidentially.

### Privacy & Confidentiality

Employees must:
- Protect customer and company data
- Use strong passwords and enable MFA
- Not share login credentials
- Report security incidents immediately
- Comply with PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws

**Confidential Information includes:**
- Customer data and contracts
- Financial information
- Product roadmaps
- Source code and technical documentation
- Employee personal information

### Social Media Policy

Employees are free to use social media but must:
- Not share confidential company information
- Clearly identify personal opinions as their own
- Not represent the company without authorization
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions

### Dress Code

**Business Casual:** Standard for office days  
**Casual Fridays:** Jeans and casual attire permitted  
**Client Meetings:** Business professional attire  
**Remote Work:** Professional attire for video calls
